About Primax Global

Primax Global, Inc. is a California-based government and commercial supplier serving the procurement needs of U.S. government agencies and institutional customers.

Our company provides a broad range of products and procurement solutions designed to support government operations, facilities, information technology, and mission requirements.

Primax Global, Inc. is an approved government vendor and is positioned to supply products through authorized government procurement channels.

Government Supply & Procurement

Federal Government Supplier

Primax Global, Inc. supplies products to U.S. government customers through federal procurement channels, including government purchasing systems and military supply programs.

Our customers and procurement channels may include the U.S. Army, U.S. Air Force, U.S. Navy, and U.S. Marine Corps, as well as other federal and government organizations.
