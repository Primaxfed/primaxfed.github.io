About Primax Global

Primax Global, Inc. is a U.S.-based government vendor focused on supplying products and services to government and institutional customers.

Our business activities include government product supply, commercial and industrial sourcing, and construction services through Miju Construction.

Our goal is to provide dependable sourcing, competitive pricing, professional project support, and reliable customer service throughout the procurement process.
