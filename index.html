About Primax Global, Inc.

Primax Global, Inc. is a California-based government and commercial supplier serving the procurement requirements of U.S. government agencies, Department of Defense organizations, and institutional customers.

We provide sourcing, procurement, and supply solutions across a broad range of commercial, industrial, technology, and operational products.

Our focus is on reliable product sourcing, competitive procurement, responsive customer service, and dependable fulfillment for government and institutional requirements.

Government & Federal Supply

Federal Government & Department of Defense Supply

Primax Global, Inc. is an approved government vendor providing products and procurement solutions through authorized federal government purchasing channels.

Our government supply activities support the procurement requirements of federal agencies and Department of Defense customers, including military organizations.

Primax Global, Inc. is positioned to supply products through government procurement programs and authorized purchasing systems, including FedMall and other federal procurement channels.

Government customers and procurement organizations may include the U.S. Army, U.S. Air Force, U.S. Navy, and U.S. Marine Corps, as well as other federal agencies and government organizations.
